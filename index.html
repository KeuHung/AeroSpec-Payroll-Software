Settings & Data Backups

Configure overtime and export or restore your local employee records.

Use Import Excel in the top right to import NGTeco reports. New imports update Employee Pay, Payroll Service Summary and Weekly History automatically. Duplicate employee/date entries are replaced rather than double-counted.

Important: review pay rates, leave hours, selected dates, overtime rules and all calculated amounts before submission. Browser storage can be cleared by browser settings; export data backups to keep a separate copy. Excel exports are generated locally.

Export Payroll Workbook

Select the weeks you want included in your Excel payroll report. Every employee will be included, using only recorded time within these dates.
